Return Policy

Our cakes and pastries are freshly baked and perishable, so we cannot accept returns once an order is delivered.

  • If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 24 hours.
  • Please keep photos of the product and your Order ID ready.
  • After we verify the issue, we will offer a replacement or a refund.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Delivery Info

  • You choose the delivery date at checkout.
  • Orders are baked fresh and delivered on the selected date.
  • Please make sure someone is available at the delivery address to receive the cake.
  • Keep your phone nearby, as our delivery partner may call you for directions.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, please keep the exact amount ready.

Track Your Order

Every order gets an Order ID when it is placed. To see your orders:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Open your Account page and go to My Orders.
  3. You will see the items, total, payment method and date of each order.

Need help with an order? Contact us with your Order ID.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the details needed to process your order: your name, phone number, email and delivery address.

  • Your details are used only to prepare, deliver and support your order.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not store your full card number, CVV or gift card PIN.

Terms of Service

  • By placing an order, you agree to provide correct delivery and contact details.
  • Prices and availability can change without notice.
  • Product photos are for reference. Handmade items may look slightly different.
  • Please consume the cake on the day of delivery or store it as advised.
  • Sweet Treats may cancel an order if the delivery details are incorrect.
